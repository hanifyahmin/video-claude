## Facts used (label + description only)
- Label: SAE 80W-90, API GL-5, MIL-L-2105, 100ml, CONVENTIONAL, "applicable for all kind of
  scooter motorbike".
- Description: protection for gears and bearings; prevents wear and micro-pitting
  ("hakisan mikro"); rust and corrosion protection; good oxidation resistance, longer oil
  life; high shear and thermal stability; smoother acceleration than other conventional
  lubricants (shown with a footnote).
- **Not used**: "sintetik" — the description says synthetic but the bottle says CONVENTIONAL.
  No other source online lists TOYO-G specs (searched).
